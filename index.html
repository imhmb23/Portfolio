Focus

I'm a creative technologist — which is a slightly evasive way of saying I sit between design and engineering, and refuse to pick.

For the last eleven years I've built things that are hard to categorise: real-time visualisations, platforms that hold up under real load, and interfaces that try to feel like objects rather than forms. I've worked inside product teams, alongside studios, and alone in a room with a compiler.

I care about craft at the level of the millisecond and the pixel — and about shipping, which is the part that separates a portfolio from a person you'd actually hire.
